**JOB-BOARD**

**1.PROJECT TITLE**

 Job Board - Job openings with role of person.

**2.Description**

List of job openings with role, company and location, a detail screen with the full description, filtering by location or type, and an application form with validation. Data from a public API or saved on the device.

**3.Features**

*we can apply for job as our wish.

*we also choose our preferrence in depend on our qualification.

*so many filters are present there.

**4.Technologies used**

*python

*Flutter

*android studio

**5.Data Source**

user entered in their own personal account with their personal information.
